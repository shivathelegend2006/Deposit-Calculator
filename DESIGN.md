## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the ethos of a high-precision financial instrument cross-bred with a Swiss typographical monograph. Drawing inspiration from Dieter Rams' functional restraint, classic editorial finance literature, and archival data visualization, the aesthetic delivers absolute clarity, discipline, and understated luxury.

### Emotional Landscape & Personality
- **Rigorous & Dispassionate:** Eliminates decorative noise, gamified badges, and faux urgency. The UI treats wealth calculation as an exact science.
- **Architectural & Tactile:** Built around hair-thin ruling lines, generous margins, tabular alignment, and crisp geometric intersections.
- **Monographic Authority:** Evokes the tactile gravitas of heavyweight uncoated cream archival paper, printed with rich carbon inks and calibrated spot-color inks.

### Key Visual Directives
- **Zero Faux Depth:** Absolutely no drop shadows, no box-shadow blurs, no skeuomorphic embossing, and no gradients across any surface or control.
- **Surface Integrity:** Depth is established strictly through spatial isolation, architectural hairline grid lines, and discrete shifts between warm cream tints.
- **Deliberate Accents:** Color is applied exclusively as semantic taxonomy (SIP in emerald, RD in cobalt, FD in crimson) or as high-contrast utilitarian state markers.

## Colors

The color system relies on a warm, archival foundation offset by dense obsidian typography and three precise functional spot colors. Gradients are strictly prohibited; every swatch is flat, unadulterated, and calculated for maximum legibility.

### Surface & Ink Palette
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF8F5` (Archival Warm Cream) serves as the primary canvas, reducing optical glare while conveying paper-like permanence.
- **Surface Elevation (Tiers):** `#F5F2EB` (Milled Warm Stone) for muted modular panels, comparative data tables, and input wells.
- **Primary Ink:** `#141413` (Carbon Obsidian) for display headers, numeric indicators, and dominant readouts.
- **Secondary Ink:** `#2B2A27` (Charcoal Umber) for explanatory body text, formula breakdowns, and secondary annotations.
- **Muted Ink / Metadata:** `#7A7870` (Muted Lead) for tabular labels, unit markers (`INR`, `USD`, `%`, `YR`), and parameter counters.
- **Hairline Dividers:** `#E6E2D8` (Bone Slate) for strict 1px structural ruling lines and perimeter borders.

### Semantic Plan Taxonomy (Pure Spot Accents)
- **SIP (Systematic Investment Plan):** `#0D7842` (Active deep emerald) with `#10B981` (High-contrast data point hover/accent).
- **RD (Recurring Deposit):** `#1D4ED8` (Active pure cobalt) with `#2563EB` (Interactive state/vector track).
- **FD (Fixed Deposit):** `#DC2626` (Active vermillion) with `#E11D48` (High-frequency data point).

## Typography

The typographical engine synthesizes three distinct voices:
1. **Newsreader (Display/Editorial):** Provides humanistic, literary authority to top-tier headings, section introductions, and philosophy notes.
2. **Inter (Body/Interface):** Rational, uninflected grotesque for labels, explanations, navigation, and instructional text.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Mathematical/Instrumental):** Calibrated for all numerical inputs, computed values, percentages, chart axes, and technical readouts.

### Typesetting Principles
- **Tabular Figures:** `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` is mandatory across all numerals, data tables, and dynamic counters to prevent layout jank during real-time calculation.
- **Labels:** Monospace labels are set in full uppercase with `+0.06em` to `+0.08em` tracking for technical precision.
- **Punctuation & Symbols:** Currency indicators (`$`, `€`, `₹`) and arithmetic operators (`+`, `-`, `=`, `×`) align horizontally with the mathematical baseline of the monospace numerals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a rigorous 12-column Swiss typographic grid. Every boundary is defined by architectural spacing or single-pixel dividers rather than floating cards.

### Architectural Grid Structure
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column grid, 1280px max-width container, 4rem margins, 2.5rem gutters. Layout separates cleanly into an interactive parameter column (5 columns) and an uninterrupted data visualization and projection yield column (7 columns).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column grid, 2rem margins, 1.5rem gutters. Controls stack naturally above the projection charts.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 1.25rem margins and 1rem gutters. Continuous vertical scroll with sticky hairline metric summaries.

### Spatial Discipline
- **Modular Rhythm:** Vertical rhythm is built on an absolute 8px module (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`, `4rem`, `6rem`).
- **Data Densities:** Control groups employ tight, compact internal spacing (`space-sm` to `space-md`), contrasted with expansive breathing room (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) between distinct analytical modules.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through plane segmentation and hairline borders. Drop shadows and surface blurs are strictly rejected.

### Surface Tiers & Hairline Framing
- **Flat Spatial Separation:** Depth exists exclusively as a 2D plane hierarchy. Subordinate panels (e.g., input matrices, yield summary trays) utilize the `#F5F2EB` surface set directly against the `#FAF8F5` background canvas.
- **Architectural Rules (1px Borders):** Component boundaries, column separators, and header anchors use a single 1px solid rule (`#E6E2D8`). Borders must never exceed 1px.
- **Active Structural Insetting:** When selecting an investment model (SIP, RD, FD), active status is designated by altering the bounding border color to the exact plan accent (`#0D7842`, `#1D4ED8`, or `#DC2626`) with a 1px solid stroke—no halo, no glow, and no drop shadow.
- **Z-Index Discipline:** Modals and floating value tooltips sit on solid `#FAF8F5` backgrounds bounded by a 1px `#141413` stroke to produce clean, sharp contrast.

## Shapes

The design system uses a strict **Sharp (`0px`)** geometry across all elements.

### Geometrical Rules
- **Absolute Right Angles:** Zero border-radius across buttons, input fields, segmented tabs, data chips, sliders, and canvas wrappers.
- **Precision Corners:** Every element terminates in crisp 90-degree intersections, echoing physical technical drawing tools, specimen ledger sheets, and calibrated drafting rulers.
- **Focus Rings:** High-contrast 1px solid `#141413` outlines with a 2px white/cream offset gap (`outline-offset: 2px`). Rounded focus rings are forbidden.

## Components

Every component functions as a calibrated drafting instrument. Decorative ornamentation is eliminated in favor of pristine typographic precision.

### Buttons & Mode Switchers
- **Plan Selector (SIP / RD / FD):** A horizontal 3-way segmented strip bounded by `#E6E2D8`. Each segment has `0px` radius, monospace label, and transparent background. The selected tab receives a solid 1px inner indicator in the plan’s accent color (`#0D7842`, `#1D4ED8`, or `#DC2626`) with text in `#141413` and a small solid square prefix (4x4px) filled with that exact accent.
- **Action Buttons:** Primary actions are rendered in solid `#141413` fill with `#FAF8F5` text, rectangular (`0px` radius), and uppercase monospace typesetting. Hover states invert to `#2B2A27`. Secondary actions feature a `#FAF8F5` surface with a 1px `#E6E2D8` border, transitioning to `#141413` border on hover.

### Input Fields & Parameter Steppers
- **Numeric Display Inputs:** Large JetBrains Mono editable digits set flush against the bottom axis, underlined by a 1px `#E6E2D8` rule. On focus, the rule becomes 1px `#141413`.
- **Unit Labels:** Affixed currency symbols (`$`, `₹`) and unit metrics (`/ mo`, `years`, `%`) sit inline in `#7A7870` monospace.
- **Range Sliders:** A single 1px `#E6E2D8` horizontal rail. The active progress track is rendered in the active plan’s pure accent color. The slider thumb is an unrounded 8x16px solid rectangular block (`#141413`), dragging effortlessly without shadow.

### Cards & Modular Containers
- **Zero-Clutter Data Cells:** "Cards" do not exist as raised drop-shadow islands. Instead, metrics are housed in modular data cells separated by 1px `#E6E2D8` vertical and horizontal rules, resembling an archival ledger or balance sheet.
- **Cell Padding:** Generous `1.5rem` to `2rem` internal clearance allowing computed numbers to command visual weight.

### Data Art & Projection Visualizations
- **Trajectory Curves:** Single hairline (1.5px) SVG vectors without gradient area fills. The path is plotted in the exact plan accent.
- **Comparison Vectors:** Secondary investment comparative curves are rendered in fine 1px dashed rules (`#7A7870`).
- **Grid Lines & Axes:** Subdued 1px hairline rules (`#E6E2D8`) with JetBrains Mono tick marks along the perimeter. Zero colored chart grid backgrounds.
- **Crosshair Hover:** A 1px vertical hairline rule tracks pointer position, displaying a clean rectangular tooltip block (solid `#FAF8F5` with 1px `#141413` border, zero shadow).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** 14x14px square boxes with 1px `#E6E2D8` border. Selected state is marked with a sharp solid 8x8px `#141413` square fill inside (no checkmark icon).
- **Radio Buttons:** Replaced by square radio markers containing a centered 6x6px solid square when active, honoring the 0px architectural geometry.
