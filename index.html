Where Do the Formulae Come From?

Formulas aligned with standard banking benchmarks & regulatory compounding conventions (RBI / Indian Banking Standards for SIP, RD quarterly compounding, and FD cumulative annual compounding).

How Each Instrument Compounds

Systematic Investment Plans channel recurring monthly sums into diversified equity mutual funds, benefiting from rupee-cost averaging across market cycles and compounding long-term equity growth.
Recurring Deposits pool regular monthly deposits at a guaranteed fixed interest rate with Indian commercial banks, compounding quarterly per banking conventions with zero equity market volatility.
Fixed Deposits lock in a lump-sum principal with banks or NBFCs at an agreed term rate, with cumulative annual compounding that preserves capital while generating predictable returns upon maturity.
01

Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)

Monthly Compounding
M = P × [((1 + i)n − 1) / i] × (1 + i)
P = Scheduled monthly installment
i = Monthly interest rate (Annual % ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
n = Total number of monthly installments (Years × 12)
02

Recurring Deposit (RD)

Quarterly Compounding
M = ∑k=1..n P × (1 + r / 400)4 × (n − k + 1) / 12
P = Monthly deposit
r = Annual bank interest rate (%)
k = Deposit installment counter from 1 to n
03

Fixed Deposit (FD)

Lump Sum Lock
A = P × (1 + r / 400)4 × t
P = Initial lump sum principal deposit
r = Annual locked bank interest rate (%)
t = Tenure duration in years

How to Use This Calculator

A step-by-step guide to projecting and comparing your investments.

Step 1

Choose an investment

Start by selecting one of the three options at the top:

SIP

Systematic Investment Plan

RD

Recurring Deposit

FD

Fixed Deposit

The colour of the calculator changes with your selection: Green → SIP Blue → RD Red → FD
Step 2

Choose what you know

Use the selector below the investment options.

I Know My Investment:

Choose this when you know how much you can invest and want to find out what it can become.

I Know My Target:

Choose this when you already have a target amount and want to find out what it takes to reach it.

The fields shown below will change automatically based on your choice.

Step 3

Enter your numbers

Enter the values requested by the calculator.

Depending on the investment and mode, these may include:

  • Investment or deposit amount
  • Target amount
  • Interest/expected return rate
  • Investment duration

You can type amounts normally. You do not need to enter ₹, %, or commas.

For example: 100000 → ₹1,00,000
For example: 7.5 → 7.5%
Step 4

Calculate

Once all required values have been entered, press Calculate Projection.

The calculator processes your inputs and displays the estimated result.

For an investment-based calculation, this will normally show:

Maturity Amount · Total Invested · Estimated Profit

For a target-based calculation, it will show the value you need to reach your target, such as:

Required Monthly Investment or Time Required

Step 5

Explore the result

Your result is more than a single number.

The calculator separates:

What you put in
from
What the investment generated

so you can see how much of the final amount came from your own contributions and how much came from estimated returns.

Step 6

Compare SIP, RD and FD

After calculating a result, select Compare to see all three investment paths together.

The comparison graph shows how the estimated value of:

SIP · RD · FD

changes year by year.

Each line uses its investment colour:

🟢SIP 🔵RD 🔴FD

Move across the graph to see the estimated amount for each investment at a particular year.

Step 7

Learn how the calculations work

Select How It Works at the top of the calculator.

This opens the explanation page, where you can explore:

  • How SIP, RD and FD work
  • The calculation method used for each
  • The mathematical formula behind each projection
  • A visual explanation of how each investment grows

Use Return to Calculator to come back and continue calculating.

You can see the exact mathematical formulae and compounding models in the How It Works page.

Step 8

A note about the results

The calculator provides mathematical estimates based on the values you enter.

Actual returns may differ because of factors such as the interest rate offered, compounding frequency, taxes, fees, product terms and market performance.

SIP returns are projections, not guaranteed returns.
Step 9

Quick start

Choose → Enter → Calculate → Explore → Compare

That's all you need to do.
